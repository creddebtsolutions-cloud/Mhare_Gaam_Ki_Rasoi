Google Play Account Deletion Policy Notice

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users of Mhare Gaam Ki Rasoi have the full right to request the permanent deletion of their account, registered phone number, saved delivery addresses, and personal profile data.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving your deletion request, the following information will be permanently removed from our active databases within 48 to 72 hours:

  • Profile Details: Your name, registered phone number, and optional email address.
  • Saved Addresses: All saved delivery locations, street names, and landmark notes.
  • App Preferences: Saved food customization choices, dietary tags, and cart contents.
  • Marketing Data: Removal from all future WhatsApp/SMS notification lists.
Statutory Retention Exception: Historical financial invoices, GST billing records, and tax payment references are retained as required by Indian commercial tax laws (GST Act & Companies Act) for mandatory statutory audit periods.
