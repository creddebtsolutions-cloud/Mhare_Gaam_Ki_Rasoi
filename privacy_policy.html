Privacy Policy

Mhare Gaam Ki Rasoi — Ghar Jaisa Swaad

● 100% Pure Vegetarian Restaurant
Last Updated: September 2026 | Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to Mhare Gaam Ki Rasoi ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate the Mhare Gaam Ki Rasoi mobile food ordering application and website for our restaurant located at Sai Plaza, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application or website.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate food delivery and dine-in reservation services, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, and optional email address for login verification, order updates, and receipts.
  • Delivery Address & Location: Physical delivery addresses, apartment details, landmark instructions, and precise or approximate GPS location (with your permission) to calculate delivery route, estimate arrival time, and dispatch delivery partners in Meerut.
  • Order Details: Items ordered, dietary instructions (e.g., Jain preference, spice level), order history, and customer reviews.
  • Payment Details: Transaction identifiers, payment method selected (UPI, Cash on Delivery, or Net Banking). Note: We do not store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers. All digital payments are processed through RBI-approved, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique push notification tokens for live order status delivery alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for restaurant operations and customer service:

  • Processing, preparing, and delivering your pure vegetarian food orders.
  • Sending transactional SMS, WhatsApp, or push notification updates regarding your order status (Placed, Preparing, Out for Delivery, Delivered).
  • Assigning orders to our delivery fleet riders in Meerut.
  • Managing customer loyalty reward points and applying discount coupons.
  • Improving menu offerings and food quality based on genuine customer feedback.

3. 100% Pure Vegetarian & FSSAI Standards

Mhare Gaam Ki Rasoi is a certified 100% Pure Vegetarian kitchen. We never handle, store, or prepare any meat, poultry, seafood, or egg items. All food is prepared under strict FSSAI food safety and hygiene guidelines.

4. Sharing of Information

We respect your privacy and never sell or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers. Information is only shared with:

  • Assigned Delivery Partners: Sharing your name, delivery address, and contact number so our delivery riders can reach your doorstep.
  • Authorized Service Providers: Secure payment gateway partners and transactional SMS gateways for OTP verification.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulations.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS / TLS) and secure database storage to protect your personal data. We retain your order history and account details only as long as necessary to fulfill service requests, manage reward points, and comply with legal or accounting requirements.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You may at any time:

  • View and update your saved delivery addresses and contact information in your app profile.
  • Opt out of promotional communications.
  • Request complete deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team at privacy@mharegaam.in.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general audiences capable of entering into food ordering contracts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent.
